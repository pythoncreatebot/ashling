隐私政策

Ashling 的目标是安静陪伴。它没有账号系统，不提供自家服务器服务，不包含统计分析或广告，也不追踪你。

Apple 健康步数

经你允许后，Ashling 只会读取 Apple 健康中的步数，用在小岛上今天的行程。步数只在这台 iPhone 上使用，不会上传。

提醒通知

Ashling 使用本机通知，每天最多一条，用来提示它带来的新东西。你可以在 App 设置或系统设置中关闭通知。

应用内购买

所有购买都由 Apple 的 App Store 处理。开发者无法获得你的付款信息。

小组件与实时活动

主屏幕小组件和锁屏实时活动需要的少量状态数据，只保存在这台 iPhone 的 App Group 里。

照片

拍立得和相册照片保存在这台 iPhone 上的应用存储里。只有当你选择分享时，照片才会交给系统的分享面板；分享给谁由你决定。

本机数据

你的游戏进度和偏好保存在这台 iPhone 上。删除 App 会删除这些本机数据。

Privacy Policy

Ashling is designed to be quiet company. It has no accounts, no first-party servers, no analytics or advertising, and it does not track you.

Apple Health steps

With your permission, Ashling only reads your step count from Apple Health to decide today’s small journey on the island. Steps stay on this iPhone and are never uploaded.

Reminders

Ashling uses local notifications, at most one per day, to let you know when it brings something back. You can turn reminders off in the app’s settings or in system Settings.

In-app purchases

All purchases are handled by Apple’s App Store. The developer does not receive your payment details.

Widgets and Live Activities

The small amount of status data needed by Home Screen widgets and Lock Screen Live Activities stays in this iPhone’s App Group.

Photos

Instant photos and album photos are stored in the app’s own storage on this iPhone. A photo is handed to the system share sheet only when you choose to share it; you decide where it goes.

On-device data

Your progress and preferences stay on this iPhone. Deleting the app deletes this on-device data.
